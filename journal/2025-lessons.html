2025

2025 Lessons

Lessons, reminders, prayers, realizations, and truths I wanted to carry with me.

  1. Persistence and patience often lead to the most meaningful victories.
  2. Secret weapon — OVER PREPARE.
  3. DOUBLE DOWN IN PRAYER.
  4. In each moment, ask:
    “How can I appreciate even more God’s grace and guidance in this moment?”
  5. You learn resiliency and tenacity during tough times, not easy ones. When tough choices have to be made and results are difficult to achieve — LEADERS ARE FORGED.
  6. Strive toward ever greater HUMILITY.
  7. BUT IN MY HEART, I BELONG TO CHRIST.
  8. I know God will turn whatever is happening to me right now into the defining moment in my life.
  9. My definition of success: FULFILLING GOD’S PURPOSE FOR MY LIFE.
  10. FIGHT TO BE FAITHFUL.
  11. Perception vs. Perspective: The way you win is when you move off your perception and into perspective. Now you’re thinking like them, which will give you informational advantage.
  12. No matter who you are, at some point, you will get rejected. And that’s fine.
  13. CONSISTENCY BUILDS CONFIDENCE.
  14. Life gets a lot better when you realize that worrying is just thinking that God is going to mess up.
  15. GOD’S WILL. GOD’S WAY. GOD’S TIME.
  16. When the time is right, I, the Lord, will make it happen.
  17. The world will bring opportunities to the people that are consistent, driven, and have strong values and character.
  18. Surrender like Mama Mary.
    “Be it done to me according to thy word.”
    Then get up and serve.
  19. God will give you strength. But you still have to pick up the weight and lift it.
  20. When we understand how deep our weakness goes, then we will understand how great God’s mercy and love is. In this season of my life, I keep hitting roadblocks after roadblocks that lead me to keep discovering the depth of my weakness and brokenness, only to find God’s mercy there.
  21. So much of what we see is a reflection of who we are.
  22. God doesn’t want you to figure it out. He wants you to trust that He already has.
  23. The best version of your answered prayer will come at the best time.
  24. Best anti-depressant: Learning not to believe every stupid thing you think.
  25. God is not bounded by time.
  26. Jesus doesn’t need your abilities. He wants your AVAILABILITY.
  27. When sin abounds, God’s grace abounds all the more.
  28. Raise your standard of giving, not your standard of living.
  29. Mentally strong people always reflect and ask:
    “Does my behavior fit the goals I have for my life?”
  30. NOTHING GREAT IS EVER ACHIEVED WITHOUT MUCH ENDURING. — St. Catherine of Siena
  31. If we allow God in our life, our greatest virtues often will rise from the ashes of our greatest vice. Do not be afraid or too frustrated with your weaknesses and brokenness. Offer everything to Jesus — and let Him turn it into something beautiful for God.
  32. Jesus is found not in grand gestures, but in the quiet faithfulness of the present moment.
  33. Begin, even poorly. Begin, even distracted. Begin, for love. The soul that walks with determination, even with a limp, will reach the heights.
  34. You don’t need more time. You just need to decide.
  35. Procrastination is not merely a habit — it is a resistance to grace. And distraction, when left unchecked, scatters the soul like dust in the wind. The devil delights when you hesitate, for in delay he finds his doorway. But God speaks now — always now. His will is found not in tomorrow, but in the faithful offering of this present moment.
  36. It is not the greatness of the task, but the purity of will that pleases God. Begin what you must, not for your glory but for His. And fear not your weakness — His strength is perfected there.
  37. God does not ask for your success — He asks for your faithfulness.
  38. You say, “I try and fail, and try again, yet still I fall.” Do not be troubled. Even your falls, when offered with humility, become steps toward holiness. The devil desires you to look at your failure and grow weary. But God desires you to look at His mercy and rise.
  39. Your task is not to measure the harvest, but to sow with love and water with trust.
  40. Even when you feel dry, weak, or unseen — continue. Every act of obedience, every effort to love, every time you begin again after falling, is a victory. The soul who clings to Christ in failure is more pleasing than the one who runs ahead without Him.
  41. It’s humbling, really — how God moves quietly while we’re busy overthinking, doubting, and questioning if things will ever work out. You pray, you cry, you wait — and then one day, out of nowhere, He provides. Not always in the way you expected, but exactly how you needed. And in that moment, you realize He heard every cry, saw every sleepless night, and remembered every prayer. He was never late. He was just waiting for the perfect moment.
  42. Your problem is not that you don’t love God enough. Your problem is that you don’t understand how much God loves you. God loves you on your good days and bad days. God loves you when you feel it and when you don’t feel it.
  43. It’s not the size of your brain that matters. It’s how many bruises, cuts, and calluses you’re willing to have that matters.
  44. WINNERS WANT PRESSURE.
  45. GREATNESS COMES FROM CHARACTER AND CHARACTER IS REFINED BY PAIN AND SUFFERING.
  46. FAITH AND REASON ARE TWO WINGS THAT ELEVATE THE HUMAN SPIRIT TOWARD THE TRUTH. AND WITH TRUTH, WE HAVE NOTHING TO FEAR.
  47. Be a risk taker. Challenge myself more and not be afraid to be more visible.
  48. The beautiful sign of detachment.
  49. Mental strength is not just hoping that nothing ever goes wrong. It is believing that we have the capacity to handle it if it does.
  50. IT’S ALL A GIFT. THAT’S WHY EVERYTHING IS SOMETHING TO BE GRATEFUL FOR.
  51. You cannot be half-hearted and succeed or be happy. If you want to live life to the fullest, you must give it your all in whatever cause or purpose you believe in that’s worth dying for.
  52. Instead of goal setting — imagine it instead and run the story in your head in full detail.
  53. The way you let people treat you is a reflection of how you value yourself. Raise your standards.
  54. Don’t settle for mediocrity.
  55. I had this realization that true peace comes from strong character and values and faith. The problems and challenges in life are inevitable. But when you are grounded by strong character and values — that you are truly honest with yourself, that you have good intentions, that you are not lying or cheating or stepping on others just to get what you want — then whatever happens, you will be at peace.
  56. Where you are being most tempted and discouraged is often the area where God is working to sanctify you. Your wounds are not random.
  57. The endurance of darkness is the preparation of great life.
  58. I cannot do everything, but I can do some things that truly matter.
  59. MIND — is a spiritual battlefield.
  60. You don’t build character in comfort.
  61. You cannot be half-hearted and succeed. GO ALL IN.
  62. Be prepared when you ask God to change you. Are you willing to sit in the pain of purification long enough to transform you? God doesn’t reveal what’s broken to shame you. He reveals it to HEAL YOU.
  63. For God, you are so much more than your dreams, goals, and achievements.
  64. Do what you have, with what you can, where you are. Jesus will meet you there.
  65. The enemy tempts us most fiercely where God is calling us most powerfully.
  66. The enemy will attack where the grace will be most fruitful.
  67. The thing you worry the most about is the thing you trust God the least. Pray and let God take it.
  68. Your feelings are not in charge. YOU ARE.
  69. “Let your struggles meet God’s miracles.”
    — Stephany Cacas
  70. The cool thing about going through hard times is realizing that you can survive them. You come out changed, yes — but once you know you can survive the worst, you stop fearing what’s next.
  71. MAGIC NOTEBOOK.
  72. Daily Goals:
    How can I honor God today?
    What is something I can do for someone today?
    What is something I can do to add value to the world today?
  73. Whatever you're not changing, you're choosing.
  74. Suffering is the greatest treasure — St. Faustina.
    For it purifies the soul.
    Blessed are the pure of heart, for they shall see God.
  75. When someone is drowning, it’s not the best time to teach them how to swim.
  76. Reminder: Done beats perfect every time.
  77. It’s okay if you are a workaholic if you love it. Especially if you thrive in chaos.
  78. Run towards the hardest problems. That’s where you find the biggest opportunities — where you learn the most and grow the most.

    When you choose the hardest challenges, you make the fastest path to growth and the greatest chance to make a difference.
